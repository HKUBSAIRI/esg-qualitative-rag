The BeOne Care Foundation

The BeOne Care Foundation*, is a nonprofit organization dedicated to eliminating barriers to accessible cancer care in under-represented communities around the world.

We believe that everyone at risk of or living with cancer should have access to interventions that can help empower them to achieve their best possible outcomes. Through co-creating innovative and transformative interventions, we can help close the gap in cancer care and treatment disparities. The BeOne Care Foundation also supports nonprofit organizations that positively impact the communities where BeOne operates and those providing response and recovery support following a natural or humanitarian disaster.

The Foundation provides funding for charitable, community-engaged programs in three strategic areas:

  • Access to cancer care
  • Community engagement
  • Disaster response

BeOne Care Foundation Access to Cancer Care Grant Program

Despite significant progress in cancer diagnosis and treatment, considerable disparities in access to cancer care remain worldwide. That’s why the BeOne Care Foundation prioritizes interventions that aim to empower patients, healthcare providers, and communities to achieve optimal outcomes.

To date, we’ve awarded over $500,000 in grants supporting cancer screening, detection, and diagnostic capabilities in underserved and high-risk communities worldwide. We believe that working with these communities, including private and public sector partners, will create innovative and transformative solutions to reduce disparities in cancer care and treatment.

In 2025, our grants helped support:

  • Frankly Speaking About Cancer, a cancer screening and support program from Cancer Support Community, a Washington, D.C.-based organization that helps people impacted by cancer navigate barriers to care, reached approximately 338,000 people in the U.S.
  • February Orange, a leukemia awareness and education campaign from ABRALE, a São Paulo-based organization dedicated to ensuring all people with blood cancer in Brazil access the best treatment, impacted over 200,000 people and supported 396 patients.
  • Leukemia is Not All the Same, an online awareness campaign from Instituto Camaleão, a Porto Alegre-based organization that supports cancer patients through recovery and reintegration, reached over 2.4 million people  and trained 62 health professionals and students in Brazil.

Partnership with BeOne and The Max Foundation

In May 2023, the BeOne Care Foundation made its first grant to The Max Foundation as part of a three-year collaboration with BeOne to deliver medicine at no cost to patients in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). During the first three years of our partnership, we have reached more than 300 patients with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) in Armenia, Ethiopia, and Nepal.

At BeOne, we aim to reach every patient who needs our medicines, regardless of where they live. Learn more about our commitment to Global Access Through Philanthropy.

“We look forward to working together with our grant partners to advance innovative and transformative solutions to reduce disparities in cancer care and treatment.”

Christine Riley Miller

Executive Director, Responsible Business and Sustainability, BeOne

The BeOne Care Foundation does not currently accept unsolicited proposals for funding.

*The BeOne Care Foundation is a charitable organization established by BeOne Medicines Ltd. It is a separate legal entity from BeOne Medicines Ltd. with distinct legal restrictions.
