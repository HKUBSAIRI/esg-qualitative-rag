Supplier Onboarding

Thank you for showing your interest in doing business with BeOne Medicines. Your BeOne Medicines contact will initiate the supplier onboarding process by sending an email invitation to complete the Supplier Registration Form. The email will be sent by either BeOne Medicines or Ariba (no-reply@cnsmtp.sapariba.cn). This helps us collect the necessary information and create your supplier record in our ERP system. Once we review and approve your registration, we’ll finalize the onboarding and issue your first Purchase Order.

Please follow the instructions carefully, fill in all required fields, and upload the necessary documents (e.g., tax ID and supporting banking information). Failure to provide all the required information can result in payment delays. Be sure to check your spam or junk folder for emails from the Ariba address. After you submit the form, we will review your information and documents. If we need more information, we’ll reach out to you directly.

Important: Please do not ship goods or begin services until you have received an official Purchase Order from BeOne Medicines.

  • Ariba Supplier Onboarding Process

    Thank you for showing interest in doing business with BeOne. BeOne uses SAP® Ariba® Network to manage its sourcing and procurement activities and to collaborate with suppliers.

    How to Become a BeOne Supplier Quick Reference Guide

    Supplier Banking Data Guideline

    Register to become a BeOne Supplier

    1. You will receive an email from BeOne, inviting you to register to become a supplier, which contains all the details required for the onboarding process. To get started, you need to create an account on the SAP® Ariba® Network (ASN). It’s free and easy to use.
    2. Create a new SAP® Ariba® Network (ANID) or log in using an existing ANID
    3. Using your Network ID (ANID), complete the BeOne Registration form

    Access the below webinars for additional information:

    • Register for an Ariba Enterprise Account
    • Register for an Ariba Standard Account

    Your registration on the Ariba Network, while helpful, is not mandatory, and is subject to Ariba’s privacy policies and notices. All services offered by the Ariba Network are their own and not under BeOne’s control. Should you prefer not to register with the Ariba Network, please contact vendors@beonemed.com who will work with you to find an alternative means of registering with BeOne’s vendor network.

    Request to Become a Qualified Supplier for BeOne

    1. Complete relevant questionnaires: BeOne’s supplier onboarding process lets you complete questionnaires to gather relevant details about your organization. BeOne will review your questionnaire feedback to assess whether you are qualified for the appropriate spend category and region.
    2. Submit your questionnaire: After you have submitted your questionnaires to SAP® Ariba® Network, our Supplier Onboarding team will start the due diligence and qualification steps to create you as a supplier. If you need to update details, please log in to SAP® Ariba® Network. BeOne’s Supplier Onboarding team will review all updates.

    Qualified Suppliers

    Once qualified, BeOne will be able to place the first Purchase Order with you.

Sourcing Collaboration

At BeOne Medicines, we recognize the critical role of strong relationships with trusted suppliers in delivering quality products and services. As we strive to advance innovative therapies that transform patients’ lives, we partner with a diverse global community of suppliers who share our dedication to integrity, compliance, and ethical standards.

Our Approach to Supplier Partnerships

We collaborate with a wide-ranging network of suppliers across various industries, ensuring that our customers receive exceptional value and service. Be it manufacturers, distributors, or service providers, we value collaboration and transparency to foster mutual growth.

Empowering Collaborative Sourcing with SAP Ariba

To enhance our sourcing activities, BeOne Medicines utilizes the SAP Ariba Network, providing a centralized, user-friendly, and secure environment for all aspects of the sourcing journey.

eSourcing is a comprehensive, integrated solution that fosters a transparent procurement process. It allows you to participate by responding to Requests for Information (RFIs), Requests for Proposals (RFPs), and eAuctions.

Are You Ready to Partner with BeOne Medicines?

Our category management team continuously seeks innovative and capable partners to help elevate our business to the next level. If you believe your company possesses the solutions and expertise we require, we invite you to share your capabilities with us. We look forward to exploring potential collaborations!

Why Partner with Us?

  • Long-Term Relationships: We prioritize building lasting partnerships that generate value for both parties. Our team collaborates closely with suppliers to ensure ongoing success and collaboration.
  • Sustainability Focused: We seek suppliers who align with our sustainability objectives. If your practices are environmentally responsible, we want to hear from you.
  • Innovation-Driven: We are looking for suppliers committed to advancing technology, products, and processes. We are eager to partner with you if you are innovative and forward-thinking.
  • Global Reach: Our extensive network spans multiple regions, providing suppliers access to global markets and opportunities.

What We Look for in a Supplier

We seek suppliers who share our values and commitment to excellence. To be a successful partner, we look for:

  • Reliability: Consistent on-time delivery and quality are non-negotiable.
  • Quality Assurance: We require high-quality materials and services that meet or exceed industry standards.
  • Competitive Pricing: While quality is paramount, we also value competitive pricing and flexibility from our suppliers.
  • Ethical Practices: We expect our suppliers to adhere to ethical business practices, including fair labor standards and environmental responsibility.
  • Certifications: Preferred suppliers should hold relevant industry certifications (e.g., ISO, Fair Trade, etc.) to ensure compliance with regulatory standards.

If your company meets these criteria and shares our vision, let’s explore how we can work together to achieve mutual growth and success.
